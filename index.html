OUR PHILOSOPHY

Where Global Craft Meets Mumbai Elegance

Located in the heart of Bandra West, AURA Atelier is Mumbai's sanctuary for discerning individuals who appreciate meticulous grooming. Founded in 2012, our studio blends European precision barbering with timeless Indian wellness rituals.

From custom beard sculpting and hair coloring to our famous warm herbal oil Champi head massage, every treatment is tailored to elevate your personal style signature. Enjoy complimentary Cutting Chai, Artisanal Espresso, or Single Malt beverages in our private lounge.
